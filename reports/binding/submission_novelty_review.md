Two bibliographic distinctions must survive a manuscript update. R4's [version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17648v3) and [v3 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.17648v3) have different titles; the notes preserve both instead of guessing a venue. R8's [supplied preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.17030v2) lists three authors, while the [ICLR conference publication](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/70b8505ac79e3e131756f793cd80eb8d-Abstract-Conference.html) includes Atticus Geiger. No change was made to the user's original reference document.

Our project conclusions remain narrower than a general binding mechanism. The generator, semantic oracle, structural grouping, matched interventions and recoverability controls are implemented. Earlier computed-program reliability gates failed; ownership-site effects were weak or nonselective. The new confidence analysis establishes a consumed-discovery association under fixed feature/access contracts. It does not establish tracking rather than reconstruction, natural causal use, unfamiliar-program prediction, or internal-measurement value beyond a strong confidence comparator.

The [confidence decision](discovery_confidence_decision.md) should therefore be presented as baseline qualification. Source interactions are consequential; oracle inputs and graded neutral responses carry stronger access; four next-token scalars use an additional target prefill. The new complete-answer candidate bundle also changes numerical precision and uses many teacher-forced continuation states. A comparison of these bundles cannot isolate a pure information effect from precision, encoding or function-class effects. Scalar confidence is not a novel general principle: the additional primary references document prior calibration and model self-evaluation work.

A defensible new central contribution still needs a stable supported claim and the evaluation appropriate to it. For the original explanatory claim, that includes selective counterfactual behavior and a genuinely frozen unfamiliar-program comparison against qualified source, confidence and behavioral baselines. For any narrower empirical claim, overlap with these sources must be assessed around its actual results, uncertainty and access costs. The present review does not predict venue acceptance. Source versions should be rechecked before a submission.
