## Direct labels and predictions

The 192 categorical columns form 24 heads: assignment_joint 16,assignment_owner 4,assignment_version 4,parent_joint 16,parent_owner 4,parent_version 4, each at the four actual copy events(statements 4..7). The parent is the defining assignment read immediately before its consumer, even if that definition becomes obsolete later. The write head identifies the actual assignment at that event, including one superseded by capture time. Four literal initialization nodes are excluded from the primary event task; their fixed owners are a prior rather than learned identity evidence.

A joint label is the fixed global code 4*owner_slot+(version-1), owner slots 0..3 and versions 1..4. Predict by argmax over fitted joint scores with the inherited deterministic tie rule; decode using this global codebook only. No per-program source/SSA map may convert a predicted statement index into identity after prediction. SSA maps and semantic traces supply ground truth and evaluation strata only. Capture vectors and character features receive none of those target fields. Future events are undefined, not negative. Retain all sixteen joint/four owner/four version candidates at a visible event, including absent or impossible domain choices; do not prune choices using the true source state.

Primary endpoints are joint exact recovery of all four write identities and all four parent identities at layer 24/site 7. Also report combined-eight identity recovery, pooled per-head rates, all head/class/graph/profile denominators, and current/obsolete copy-definition strata where defined. Keep global-codebook identity correctness separate from scalar-component accuracies and joint-versus-factorized consistency. A joint 16 head has sixteen outputs versus eight for separate owner 4+version 4 heads combined; report this capacity difference rather than interpreting any difference as an association mechanism.
