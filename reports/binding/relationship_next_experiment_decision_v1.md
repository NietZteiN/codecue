# Decision after the overnight relationship suite

The October 27–28 suite completed all14 declared worker stages. Keep the [original plan](overnight_20261027_plan_v1.md), [consolidated results](overnight_20261027_findings_v1.md), completion receipts and failed earlier reliability gates. No result here qualifies the original causal binding-interference claim or opens the sealed unfamiliar-program evaluation.

The useful empirical distinction is between decoder accessibility for dependency structure and for scalar values. On the familiar four-root/four-copy scaffold, dependency graphs decode well in both literal and computed-root programs. Numeric relations and answer generation weaken substantially for the computed format. On literal programs, the strong source-value pipeline exceeds direct hidden-relation decoding. Direct relation decoding can beat a pipeline derived from independently decoded hidden values, but that comparison has different total parameter counts and is not evidence for a dedicated relation code.

The [completed posthoc exact-case score/answer analysis](overnight_score_behavior_findings_v1.md) shows that dependency-graph decoding often remains correct when generated answers are wrong, with weak queried-value decoding. Both mean matched-query intervals include zero. This does not select a causal mechanism claim. Its matched-body, fixed-target query contrast is more informative about query-dependent accessibility than comparing different queried slots or separately fitted cohorts. A pre-query site is a placebo: future query text cannot explain earlier activations. Keep invalid responses, source comparators and zero-support conditional cells visible.

The next useful model assay should isolate root computation, copying and answer selection under a reliable difficulty level. Use matched root expressions and literal controls, direct questions immediately after the root calculation, and the same values carried through copies to the final query. Verify generated answers and parser behavior in the arithmetic-only controls before treating a copy-chain failure as a binding failure. Hold operation count, magnitude and surface properties fixed where feasible; report unavoidable differences. Start from the existing restricted generators and three instruction variants, rather than expanding the model list or launching optional training.

For broader relationship recovery, build an independently generated discovery topology cohort with varied overwrite schedules and explicit node/version correspondence. The current81graphs/27components, weighting seeds and numeric draws cannot supply that replication. Separate topology generalization from literal-to-computed format transfer. Any new causal assay needs verified functional counterfactuals, wrong-version/position/random/sham controls and preserved unrelated outputs; a decoded graph alone does not meet those requirements.

Keep H1 binding interference, H2 value computation/accessibility, H3 failure to use a binding, H4 query reconstruction and H5 harmless representational change as competing explanations. The completed score analysis identifies associations and corrects simple aggregate comparisons. Selecting a causal mechanism and making frozen predictions on unfamiliar programs remain later scientific decisions. Optional training, explanation-transfer and unlearning extensions remain deferred.
