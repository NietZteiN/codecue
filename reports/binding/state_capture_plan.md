# Discovery state/query captures

Prerequisites have completed: both competition runs passed their full audits, and the selected Llama 3.1 8B instrumentation candidate passed its 128-case Transformers SDPA replay against the vLLM ownership controls. All 128 parsed predictions matched and were correct. Raw decoder-block vectors at blocks 8, 16 and 24 were exactly identical before the two later query choices. A separate actual-tokenizer check verified every selected pre-query boundary. These are software checks, not a causal mechanism result.

Capture all 9,216 version/snapshot discovery cases for that checkpoint. Exclude ownership controls from this representation bank because they lack the saved-copy role. Preserve train/development assignments and every naming, lexical, available-binding, version and tail-placement condition. The plan follows the observed discovery behavior and is exploratory; no generated final-test or extrapolation data enter.

At raw decoder blocks 8, 16 and 24, capture two sites: the end of the final state assignment before the print query, and the end of the explicit query line. Do not apply final model normalization to these vectors. Labels are the oracle's current binding and saved binding, both defined and visible before the query. They are not the generated answer or an execution suffix. Store exact float32 conversions of the BF16 block outputs, input-token hashes, visible token prefixes, character/token positions, structural groups and all condition metadata.

The two query choices on each state prefix are adjacent in a batch, with identical token-prefix hashes and full prompt lengths. The extractor requires their pre-query vectors to be exactly equal at every captured layer. This both checks causality/bookkeeping and makes duplicate state observations explicit. Later pre-query decoding must deduplicate by `state_prefix_sha256`; treating both queries as independent observations would overstate the sample size.

Generate and save the matched greedy Transformers answer during the same forward pass/cache execution. This establishes the capture backend's behavior instead of assuming the earlier vLLM decisions are numerically identical. Use the same prompt IDs, BF16 precision, EOS/blank-line stopping and 16-token direct-readout ceiling. No language-model weights are trained.

Each complete paired chunk is written atomically as an NPZ containing numeric vectors and JSON metadata; pickle is disabled. Resumption checks committed chunks before loading the model. The post-run CPU audit verifies complete coverage, tensor shapes and finiteness, split/role/target metadata, visible prefixes, answer scores and exact paired pre-query invariance. About one GiB of raw vectors is expected, within the separate activation-storage default; there is no compute-time budget.

Later probes must train only on neutral training cases, use at least three fit seeds, fit all normalization/regularization choices on training data, preserve structural groups, include matched surface information and shuffled-label/selectivity controls, and report both queried and unqueried values. A successful decoder establishes recovery for that measurement; a failed decoder does not establish absence. Causal value/ownership interventions remain a subsequent validated assay and are not claimed by these captures.

Configuration: `configs/binding/competition/state_capture_v1.json`. Immutable source and submitted jobs: `artifacts/binding/state_capture/source_v1.json` and `jobs_v1.json`. Audit output will be `artifacts/binding/state_capture/llama31_8b_v1/activations/audit.json`.
