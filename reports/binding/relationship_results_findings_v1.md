## The stronger source-only pipeline matters

The source-text categorical-value pipeline scores **98.108%** on the same algebraic cells, exceeding hidden direct decoding. It predicts four values from visible source text and derives the relations from those predictions; it does not receive oracle values as features. The original direct source-text relation heads score 58.733%, so they are not the strongest source-only comparator.

The original hidden-direct versus hidden-value-derived contrast supports a difference in accessibility under the tested decoder pipelines. It does not show that the hidden representation contains more information than the source, that it beats every source-only method, or that relationships have a dedicated code. The pipelines have different total parameter counts: 18 direct primary outputs versus 128 categorical value outputs. Initial literals are visible and the body only copies values; the additive labels are not evidence of arithmetic execution.
