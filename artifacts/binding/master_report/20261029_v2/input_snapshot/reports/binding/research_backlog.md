## Competing explanations remain live

- **H1 — binding interference:** behavioral errors and some literal transfers are consistent with it; no general selective ownership/version mechanism is identified.
- **H2 — computation/accessibility:** arithmetic controls and computed-task failures are measured. The matched queried/unqueried study shows a large accessibility gap under the fixed decoder and site; its lower unqueried score does not establish that the other value was discarded.
- **H3 — failure to use available bindings:** queried-value recovery and matched query-dependent accessibility exist; neither probe success nor answer steering establishes natural causal use of that information.
- **H4 — reconstruction during answering:** explicit early/late timing now changes behavior with instruction-specific reversals; the common-final-query control sharply reduces the aggregate gap, and timing-coupled selective route tests are still needed to identify an algorithm.
- **H5 — harmless representational change:** equivalent renaming/reordering and copy-site transfers are now measured. Appropriate alignment, calibrated equivalence tests and fresh transformation validation have not settled this alternative.

The plan requires choosing an evidence-supported primary account after discovery. None of H1–H5 has been established as the general explanation.
