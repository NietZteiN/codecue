October 31, 2026. The full frozen regression passed **2,467 tests**, and all three cache workers completed. Their output/stamp files were empty, and a direct write+fsync probe reproduced `EDQUOT`; `/scratch` passed the same probe. The supervisor's write also failed, leaving its report stale. Scheduler history preserves these failures as jobs486529–486535.

All six worker directories now resolve to writable scratch directories, so subsequent parameters and scores use scratch. `/work` write checks pass again. The source snapshot and scientific protocol remain `8c1bda5687b078517a933459`.

The first worker had also saved one additional model and its scores before the quota prevented its completion JSON. Recovery job486553 verified all65 committed banks, reapplied the orphan's saved parameters with **zero score difference**, and reconstructed its missing JSON. A separately reconstructed already-committed bank matched its original metadata exactly. Recovery made zero fits and zero language-model calls. Thus66 existing banks were preserved for resume, including the recovered bank.

Following this explicit environmental-failure review, six new attempts were submitted as jobs486564–486569. Original failed attempts remain in the ledger. Supervisor486563 was installed before resubmission. The first restarted worker advanced to112 saved stages, beyond its preserved prefix; the other workers were queued at this check. No completed validation, cache or committed fit was restarted from scratch.

The operational recovery source is retained as `scripts/195_binding_numeric_storage_recovery.py` (the `recover` command).
