# Alternate-semantics postrun review

Replayed 21 selected banks and 9,629,928 saved scores; verified 108 CV banks and recomputed TRAIN selections. Maximum absolute replay difference: 0. Independent value/sign/parity/modulo metrics agree for real, shuffled and prior panels.

The parity invariant holds at 231,228 archived value coordinates. Addition and subtraction are identical modulo two, so swapping these operators preserves parity at every write. Identical parity scores are a mathematical sanity check, not an independent semantic-recovery finding.

The alternate targets have slightly lower raw scalar MAE for both hidden readouts. Source features also find them easier. The positive source-corrected normalized-MAE interaction does not establish a raw ordinary-semantic advantage, a native execution mechanism, or causal downstream use. Target scaling and learnability differ. Keep the paper claim exploratory and test global versus query-specific access next.
