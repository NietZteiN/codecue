# Decision after literal-value routing discovery

The corrected assay completed 432 full baseline responses and 20,160 intervention forwards, with 392/432 correct complete baseline answers. All 432 digit-boundary checks, 576 generation/replay matches and the complete tensor, identity and norm audits passed. The regression suite passed 213 tests. [Every registered layer and condition](value_transfer_results_v2.md) remains reported.

The first run measured a formatting space instead of the digit. Its apparent zero transfer is invalid as an answer-transfer result. The [correction](value_transfer_boundary_correction.md) preserves that diagnostic and adds exactly the same fixed space token to every input. It does not choose cases by accuracy. The earlier prefill probe measurements remain measurements at their originally recorded site.

At raw block 0, literal transfer produces the replacement digit on 144/144 affected version queries and 0/144 unchanged-answer controls. Block 8 has the same rates; block 16 reaches 93.75% on affected queries, again with zero replacement on unchanged-answer controls. This is evidence of a causal contribution from the literal-site vector to these next-token outcomes, conditional on this small program/interface family.

Ownership is less specific across all cases: block-0 replacement is 118/144 (81.94%) for affected queries and 27/144 (18.75%) for unchanged-answer controls. The latter effects occur in swap templates. In the secondary subset where both full baseline answers were correct, these are 112/112 and 0/112. This descriptive subset does not replace the full-cohort result or establish general owner selectivity.

All 576 final-block answer-vector transfers copy the donor first digit. All 2,880 final-block earlier-token controls and 2,880 identity shams retain the recipient digit. The largest relative effective-norm mismatch is 0.0296% (fixed tolerance 3%). These are software/boundary checks, independent of the literal-routing effects.

The assay uses directly assigned digits, two templates per family, two neutral lexical realizations and nine cyclic numeric triples. A post-block single-position patch leaves information at other positions intact. It neither establishes a general binding register nor explains the larger competition errors, computed values or held-out prediction. The final answer-vector effect is a software control for answer copying. No language-model training or generated final/extrapolation evaluation was performed.

The next scientific step is a separately registered test of computed intermediate values and of causal paths through the swap templates that fail behaviorally. Establish a reliable computed-value baseline first; retain the original failed arithmetic gates. Use owner/version controls and compare effects on descendants rather than selecting a favorable probe layer. No evidence-supported mechanistic forecaster is registered.
