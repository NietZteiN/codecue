# Pilot decision

The implemented study has reproducible discovery data, model runs, controlled probe fits and a bounded intervention assay. The present evidence supports a measurement and task-calibration limitation. It does not establish variable-binding interference or justify confirmatory mechanism prediction. Numerical results are generated from saved artifacts in the [pilot report](pilot_report.md); run identities and resource accounting are in the [handoff](handoff.md).

## What the evidence establishes

Independent Python execution agrees with the restricted interpreter on the generated cohorts. Naming relatives preserve semantics, structural relatives stay grouped across splits, and reserved structural outcomes remain unopened. These are engineering validations, not evidence about a transformer mechanism.

The larger short-answer discovery cohort performs near floor. The same primitive arithmetic and update failures occur under CPU FP32, CPU BF16 and GPU BF16 with matched prompt token IDs. Their agreement makes a CPU-only or BF16-only explanation insufficient for these fixtures. The uncached full-prefix decoder exactly reproduces every saved batch-one GPU response, including those failures; no generation-wrapper or cache discrepancy was detected on this suite. Shared prompt, tokenizer and checkpoint assumptions remain. Longer answers vary with batch size, so deterministic decoding must not be treated as independent replication. The uncached-forward results belong to the implementation audit rather than a fresh accuracy cohort.

Printed-output and explicit-reasoning readouts have separate identities. Neither the original reasoning budget nor its longer sensitivity passes the fixed gate on the original training suite. The sign-balanced calibration bank is validated but its conditional inference stages correctly skipped model loading. No bank accuracy exists.

State probes fit training data substantially better than development data. The complete regularization grid is reported, including surface, majority and randomized-label controls. This gap concerns the tested estimator and its generalization; it cannot establish that the model lacks the state. The prediction-prior shuffle also shows why compatibility with a wrong-binding output alone is insufficient to identify a program-specific failure mechanism. Its reference ranges describe this cohort, not population uncertainty or a causal test.

The fixed value-transfer assay passes self-patch consistency and does not repair final answers. Its late quantity queries and full-residual transfers do not isolate a naturally used pathway. Ownership and path-specific follow-ups therefore lack a demonstrated effect to localize.

## Final calibration and stopping rule

The [fresh training-only difficulty ladder](difficulty_ladder_plan.md) is now complete. Its saved plan fixed statement counts, dependency depths, answer signs, both existing protocols, a query cap and the selection/stopping rule before outcomes. This was a new calibration plan; it did not override the failed original gate or execute the existing conditional bank. All responses parsed, but no regime passed the full rule. The generated [pilot report](pilot_report.md) retains all cell results and sign breakdowns.

The stopping rule therefore ends this checkpoint/protocol feasibility pilot. Some independent-arithmetic cells perform well under reasoning, while the minimal computed baseline and dependency chains remain unreliable. Those contrasts are descriptive training results, with changed literal cues and repeated structures; they do not identify a binding mechanism. Do not relax the gate after observing the result or automatically search further prompts. Further model work requires a newly specified scientific plan. Existing code, datasets and raw runs remain reusable, and final model outcomes remain sealed.

## Paper and extension decision

No explanatory hypothesis or C predictor is currently supported, so final-outcome scoring remains premature. The forecaster and registration software is ready for evidence that may emerge later. Keep final test and reserved extrapolation outcomes sealed.

The [repository audit](repository_audit.md) separates the original CodeCue cue-conflict experiment from the new versioned-state study. The [targeted full-text comparison](related_work_comparison.md) now covers the four closest binding, state-tracking and code-reasoning sources. It shows that probe/behavior gaps, assignment-chain patching, mixed retrieval explanations and internal correctness monitoring are already close precedents. The remaining literature review and actual binding/version-specific evidence are still required before a novelty claim. The current implementation and null/mixed pilot do not by themselves establish a publishable scientific contribution.

A second model family could eventually test a concrete generalization claim once measurement is reliable; it is not part of this one-checkpoint budget. Targeted training has no demonstrated mechanism to target. Explanation transfer remains a possible section contingent on a reproducible causal effect and calibrated transfer controls. Separate explanation-transfer, unlearning and large training projects remain deferred.
