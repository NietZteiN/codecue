# Exploratory roadmap: from local structure to execution state

Recorded October 31, 2026, after the cached-state suite completed. This is an adaptive discovery plan, not a registration made before the existing DEV outcomes. Preserve all completed experiments and their original interpretations. New confirmation data must come from genuinely new grouped structural families and remain unopened until a measurement is fixed.

## Questions and competing explanations

We recover local parents substantially better than complete numeric states. Four explanations remain compatible with this: the readout discards information, the measurement site is premature, execution is query-dependent, or the model often fails to compute/retain the required value. Success on a copied literal does not validate a probe of a computed intermediate. Failure of a probe does not establish absence of information.

The current numeric features are 64-dimensional random projections. The right-operand use and computed-definition descriptors also end at the same physical token. They therefore have identical causal input at each layer, despite different numeric questions. Role conditioning permits different decoders, but adds no computation time. These are hypotheses about measurement limitations, not explanations established by the existing results.

## Ordered experiments and decisions

| ID | Question | Comparison and controls | What the outcome would resolve |
|---|---|---|---|
| E0 | How robust are the existing gains, and which failures dominate? | Saved predictions only: paired structural-family bootstrap; same-capacity hidden/source dependency comparison; numeric real/shuffled labels; depth and initial-literal-match strata; predicted/oracle routing crossed with decoded/oracle node values. | Establish uncertainty and prioritize a bottleneck without another fit. Oracle substitutions are evaluator diagnostics, not neural interventions. |
| E1 | Does compression hide numeric information? | Reuse raw archived layers. Compare 64/256/1024-dimensional nested random projections and unprojected states, one role at a time, with grouped TRAIN selection and strong ridge regularization. Match features, samples, targets and sites within each comparison. Include hashed source features of declared width, role/position priors, and coherent-family controls. | Improvement with feature width supports a measurement limitation. A null remains specific to the tested readouts. Higher width also increases decoder capacity; report parameter counts and control performance. |
| E2 | Is the numeric code easier to read in another format? | At fixed sites/features, compare scalar, categorical, sign, absolute-magnitude bins, signed decimal components, parity and residues mod 2/5/10. Evaluate a separately declared joint reconstruction of sign/digits, with exact value and MAE alongside component accuracy. | Identify accessible partial numeric properties. Digit/residue success alone does not establish full-value recovery. Retain class priors, balanced accuracy, support and all predeclared heads. |
| E3 | When does a computed value become accessible? | New captures at the RHS end, assignment delimiter/newline, later use, and query; retain all nominated layers. Compare direct answer, explicit intermediate query, and stepwise execution as separate protocols. Sample matched cases and record behavior under each protocol. | Separate premature sampling, retention and query-conditioned computation. A printed/gold intermediate is a labeled copying positive control, never evidence of latent computation. |
| E4 | Is the difficulty arithmetic, binding, or retention? | A matched factorial bank: one-hop arithmetic with no overwrite; copies/overwrites without arithmetic; the combined task; increasing delay and interference. Match target magnitudes/operators where feasible. Include same-value/different-expression and same-value-multiset/different-binding pairs. | Identify which operation first breaks and whether decoders follow meaning across surface changes. Avoid attributing a depth effect to binding when arithmetic difficulty changes too. |
| E5 | Are relations accessible without accurate absolute values? | Direct pair/triple-conditioned relation heads versus relations derived from decoded values, on identical families and sites. Match head capacity and feature access. Include order/equality/addition/subtraction, source comparators and coherent controls. | Test a relational representation separately from a numeric reconstruction pipeline. Externally applying an operator remains an evaluator calculation. |
| E6 | Does decoded information explain behavior? | On matching I0 cases, compare model answers with parent correctness, binding correctness and numeric error. Fit error predictors using grouped TRAIN cross-fitting; compare input-only difficulty features, output confidence, and added probe signals. Evaluate log loss/Brier score and clustered intervals on untouched new families. | Test incremental predictive value. Descriptive agreement on inspected DEV is not an independent error-prediction result. |
| E7 | Does a candidate representation participate in computation? | After a successful readout and behavioral positive control, freeze natural-donor, token-layout-matched interventions. Change one binding/value; use equal-value donors, unrelated-variable patches, sham patches and counterfactual output checks. | Selective changes consistent with the semantic counterfactual would support causal involvement. Broad answer steering or patch failure alone is inconclusive. Existing intervention gates still apply. |

E0 starts now. E1/E2 can reuse archived captures without more language-model inference. E3/E4 need new model runs; draft and validate their matched datasets before capture. E5/E6 follow the measurement diagnostics. E7 is a gated follow-up, not an automatic patch sweep.

## Exploration with a confirmation boundary

- Select representation site, hyperparameters and target encoding using structural TRAIN folds. Keep numeric siblings, renamings and counterfactual relatives together. Fit all normalizers/projections that learn parameters on TRAIN only.
- Report three fitting/weighting seeds, their spread, full planned panels and real/control differences. The fitting seeds are not three independent datasets. Bootstrap structural families, preserving paired conditions and averaging fitting seeds within a family.
- Existing DEV is a discovery cohort. Confidence intervals describe uncertainty within that cohort; they do not remove adaptive selection bias. Avoid declaring a winner significant after an unrestricted search. An independent generation seed alone is insufficient when the structural skeleton is reused.
- Establish behavior and copying/calculation positive controls. For target values, separate literals, computed results matching an initial literal, computed results absent from all initial literals, and unseen TRAIN computed values. Keep invalid generations in behavior denominators.
- Freeze one primary measurement and a small set of scientific contrasts before generating/opening new confirmation families. Choose confirmation size from a useful effect and structural-family variance estimated during discovery. Preserve the existing sealed final bank.
- For new task families, expand in order to aliases/containers, branches/scopes and liveness/counterfactual influence only after a useful straight-line assay exists. Syntactic dependencies and functional influence differ: `x - x` has syntactic reads but no value dependence.

## First diagnostic artifact

`scripts/190_binding_state_diagnostics.py` reads pinned source/layout/result/prediction files, writes its own immutable completion receipt, and makes no fits or model calls. Its bootstrap uses 2,000 structural-family draws with fixed RNG seed 731. Intervals are exploratory percentile intervals, not multiplicity-adjusted discovery claims. Numeric strata and oracle substitutions are evaluator analyses on existing DEV. It does not access sealed final outcomes.

## Literature informing the design

- [Hewitt and Liang, Designing and Interpreting Probes with Control Tasks](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1275/): motivation for reporting task/control differences and controlling decoder capacity.
- [Voita and Titov, Information-Theoretic Probing with Minimum Description Length](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.14/): motivation for sample efficiency/description-length analyses after a reliable assay, rather than accuracy alone.
- [Zhu, Dai and Sui, Language Models Encode the Value of Numbers Linearly](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.03735): related evidence for linear numeric decoding in a different arithmetic setting; a replication target, not a guarantee for these programs.
- [Baeumel et al., Modular Arithmetic: Language Models Solve Math Digit by Digit](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-ijcnlp.86/): motivation for testing digit-position/component representations. Transfer to our models and multi-assignment programs is an open hypothesis.
