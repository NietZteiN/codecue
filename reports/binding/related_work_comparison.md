# Closest-work comparison

Reviewed October 25, 2026. This is a targeted reading of the four closest sources in `dependvar.txt`, covering methods, relevant results and selected appendices. It is not an exhaustive novelty search or a reproduction of their experiments. Source locations and the downloaded proceedings PDF hash are in `artifacts/binding/literature_sources.json`.

| Primary source and sections inspected | Established overlap | Consequence for this study |
|---|---|---|
| [Wu et al., ICML 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.20896v2), §§2–3 and Appendices G–J; proceedings PDF also checked | A task-trained transformer follows assignment chains. Counterfactual root-value changes support residual and attention interventions; the paper also examines value/name subspaces, longer chains and weak full-state probing. | Assignment-chain patching and a probe/behavior gap are already prior art. Our proposed distinction is version-specific overwriting and competing computed bindings in a pretrained checkpoint, with registered failure forecasts. That distinction still needs empirical support. |
| [Gur-Arieh et al., ICLR 2026](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/file/2eeff35664016c7f0f8aa704f0d9a83e-Paper-Conference.pdf), §§2–5 | Counterfactual entity tasks distinguish positional, lexical and reflexive retrieval. A fitted mixture predicts intervention-conditioned output distributions; ablations and text-padding experiments assess its scope. | Generic competing retrieval explanations, causal-model prediction and longer-context tests are established precedents. A new result would need assignment-version and downstream-computation specificity rather than another broad retrieval-mixture description. |
| [Li et al., arXiv:2503.02854v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.02854v3), §§2.3–5 | Permutation tasks relate probing and prefix interventions to candidate algorithms, then examine length transfer and training curricula. The paper explicitly treats its signatures as insufficient to uniquely identify an algorithm. | State-tracking probes and intervention curves are not new by themselves. Our frozen predictors would need to explain unfamiliar-program failures beyond input features, confidence and inexpensive behavioral queries. Failed state decoding cannot supply that account. |
| [Chen et al., arXiv:2606.17648v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.17648v3), §3 and Appendices C.5, E–G | Code-answer probes are paired with context-stripped decoding. The inspected version includes transfer, downstream intervention controls, matched loop/unrolled comparisons and a detector using internal readout signals without target ground truth. | This is closer than an availability-versus-use comparison alone suggests. Adding probes, a patch or a correctness monitor would not distinguish our study. Binding/version ownership, specific counterfactual descendants and incremental out-of-structure prediction remain the proposed unfulfilled contribution. |

The final source's landing page uses a different title from the inspected HTML and PDF title, although both identify the same arXiv ID and version. Cite the versioned ID when tracking this comparison and recheck bibliographic metadata before submission; no assumption about the reason for that discrepancy is needed.

The original local CodeCue manuscript studies misleading identifiers, trace writes and recoverable answer digits. The [repository audit](repository_audit.md) records the inspected local assets. The new generator and version labels provide a different experimental substrate, but an implementation difference does not establish a scientific contribution. Current near-floor behavior, weak probe transfer and inconclusive value-transfer results do not meet the proposed explanatory standard.

Our assessment is that a defensible mechanism paper would require a reproducible, selective binding-version effect and forecasts that improve on the declared behavioral alternatives on unfamiliar structures. The fixed training ladder only tests whether this checkpoint/protocol offers a usable starting regime. Its outcome cannot validate a mechanism, establish novelty or replace the remaining full-text literature review.
