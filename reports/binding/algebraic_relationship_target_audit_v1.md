# Prefix-visible algebraic relationship TARGET audit

Prepared numeric oracle targets only; no decoder, activations, model response or new model query. Only current values after each visible assignment are used; hidden outputs, future assignments and output relevance are excluded. Equality/order and additive/subtractive identities are boolean tasks; signed difference is a separate numeric regression target. Constant/all-false boolean cells can be solved by a trivial prior and do not establish a useful decoding task. Each structural group has equal weight after deduplicating current numeric states within that group; numeric/naming relatives are not independent replications. A later direct relation probe must be compared with relations derived from independently decoded values. Relation recovery alone cannot establish a dedicated relational code, causal use, ownership or mechanism C. Numeric oracle values and all relations here are TARGETS, not readout/prediction features.

1,944 value-free source rows and 27 structural groups; 15,552 assignment-prefix views. No group crosses training/development.

Boolean cells: 776; both-class support: 712; all false: 64; all true: 0.

Signed-difference cells: 136. Their integer target distributions are reported separately from boolean classification.

Every ordered slot-pair/triple, split, visible slot frame and prefix length remains in the compact JSON. Support/prior weights are over structural groups, not row counts. Repeated naming and numeric states are deduplicated within each group.

This is target preparation. Later direct relation probes require a matched comparison with relationships derived from independently decoded values; success alone would not identify a dedicated relational representation.
