The approximately 232 MB of new source JSONL includes oracle traces. New seeds do not establish independent structures. For example, competition cohorts 1 and 2 share no exact source or canonical AST, but share 103 alpha skeletons, including 48 crossing their training/development boundaries. Existing index-based template/base labels can also change meanings across seeds. The audit preserves each bank's splits and does not silently pool them. A genuinely new family or independently sampled structural confirmation remains separate work.

Precision scenarios reuse completed discovery OOF predictions. The three fitting partition seeds are averaged before scoring; individual rows or seeds are not treated as independent structural observations. Simulated effects are hypothetical shifts of a centered empirical group distribution, with no guarantee of future power, no model-refit uncertainty and no chosen confirmatory sample size.

The next-token measurement is a new Transformers BF16/SDPA prefill backend at the exact original token boundary. It exports entropy, maximum next-token probability, top-two logit margin and EOS mass, plus vocabulary size. It exports no argmax token identity, target answer or correctness label. This is a sign/format distribution measurement; it is separate from full integer-answer event confidence. Both full runs are scheduled through SLURM and automatically audited in their own job. Both collections are complete and audited. Mean inference-window GPU utilization was 94.5% on the A30 (8B) and 94.9% on the H200 (34B); peak device memory was 16,413 MiB and 66,629 MiB respectively. Current scheduler state is in the readiness report and experiment log.

The candidate helper sums the probability of the complete canonical numeral and its explicit EOS. Alternative declared EOS tokens are disjoint variants. Prefix-free validation prevents the numeral `1` from being counted as a prefix event overlapping `16`. Its finite-support conditional entropy does not describe omitted answer strings, whitespace variants or the full output distribution. The later versioned tokenizer boundary checks and FP32 event collections completed; their qualification and changes are recorded in the follow-up above.

## Reproduction and automatic bookkeeping

Configurations are under `configs/binding/preparation/`; scripts 121–127 expose the individual tools. All model/tokenizer loading must use `sbatch`. The CPU-only readiness report can be refreshed directly:

```bash
PYTHONPATH=src ../envs/probe-cu129/bin/python scripts/124_binding_readiness.py configs/binding/preparation/readiness_v3.json
```

The collection source is pinned by `artifacts/binding/preparation/source_collection_v1.json`. It is distinct from the later release/preflight source pointer. Confidence collection and audit must use that collection snapshot because its prepared-input reviews pin the source hash. Do not rerun confidence preparation under changed source into the immutable v1 paths; use a new version for changed identities. `jobs_v1.json` records accepted scheduler IDs immediately and guards against duplicate submissions.

Every dataset/review and run lifecycle feeds the existing durable automatic logger. A dependent CPU completion job refreshes readiness and the log after the submitted jobs terminate, including failure. The periodic CPU reconciler remains enabled independently of VSCode terminal lifetime.

Those remaining items stay explicit in the readiness inventory and original checklist.
