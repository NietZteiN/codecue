# Decision after the saved-score query and error review

Close this posthoc analysis as completed. The saved decoders show query-dependent accessibility at the final query boundary, with substantial differences across graph families. The results do not establish general query tracing, reliable parent reconstruction, natural variable/version tracking or causal use. The original nine scientific decisions remain open.

An independent JSON-only review checked all **90 saved-bank diagnostic cells**, **540 model/backend/layer/site/seed/method/query-task cells**, **2,160 fixed-target-slot cells** and **810 joint-correctness pair tables**. It verified 7,290 categorical/group-count summaries, the completed receipt's scientific-metadata SHA256 pins, the prediction-replay proof and every primary three-seed mean. This review opened no NPZ, raw scores, prediction arrays, activations, coefficients, model or tokenizer; it performed no new fit, parameter application, bootstrap or LM query. Exact case alignment and prediction replay remain established by the pinned original audit, rather than a second raw-array replay in this interpretation review.

Each bank has 696 DEV rows within five canonical graph families. Matching the identical eight-assignment body, numbers and lexical form gives 174 bodies with all four final queries. Each fixed target slot contributes one queried decision and three other-query decisions: 696 fixed-target units and 2,088 other-query decisions per pooled comparison. These dependent views and the three weighting seeds are not independent replications.

The Llama definition effect changes sign under equal-graph weighting. Its graph-mean effects are approximately +2.373, +7.002, −7.407, +0.154 and −8.796 pp across the five fixed families. The two largest families contain 576/696 rows (82.759%), so a positive row-weighted average does not describe a uniformly positive graph effect. The three Llama equal-graph definition effects are +0.046, −2.558 and −1.493 pp. CodeLlama's corresponding graph effects are nonnegative in this bank, but five familiar families still do not establish population-wide query tracing. Both models' two largest families have zero fixed-target version effect; smaller families account for the positive equal-graph version effects.

The exact-prefix surface comparator has site8 definition effects of −0.383 pp by rows / −0.880 pp by graphs for Llama and −0.351 / −0.039 pp for CodeLlama. Its version effects are zero. TRAIN-prior effects are zero. Sham effects vary by model, task and weighting; their marginals and joint-label lawfulness differ from the real task. The retained controls therefore describe this feature map and decoder, without proving matched task difficulty or a selective natural mechanism. The “other three queries” comparator also need not be semantically irrelevant to a target's descendants.

The original version domain remains restricted: DEV has no version3 examples, version4 occurs only in slot0, and slot1 is always version2. Definition and parent heads predict statement indices; converting those predictions to natural variable@version labels using source maps would be an oracle-assisted postmetric. Query sensitivity of a saved linear decoder does not distinguish maintained state from query-time reconstruction or identify a natural computation pathway.
