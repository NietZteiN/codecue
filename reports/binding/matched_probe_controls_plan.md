# Frequency-matched grouped probe controls

The earlier identifier-stem controls have only two effective labels per canonical head, compared with up to 32 value labels. Their higher accuracy does not establish negative semantic selectivity. This follow-up repairs the label-frequency mismatch on the existing audited 9,216-case capture bank, without collecting new language-model responses.

This is a post-hoc measurement study. The old value-readout results are known; the control definition, seeds and full fitting matrix are fixed before new shuffled-control fits. It does not reopen final or extrapolation outcomes or begin optional language-model training.

Each numeric base program has one current/saved target pair, shared by all naming, lexical, update-count, tail-placement and query relatives. For seeds 11, 29 and 47, permute complete target pairs among the eight numeric bases within each structural-template/split stratum. Keep train and development mappings separate, and retain every randomly fixed point. This preserves the joint current/saved target distribution and the exact class frequencies and entropies in every analyzed split/naming/head cell. Whole relatives share their assigned control target; individual-row randomization is prohibited. Training and development templates must remain disjoint.

The neutral-training weighted ridge procedure remains fixed at regularization 100. Surface character-ngram and hidden features each have 4,096 dimensions, with identical 32-class head capacity. Normalization is fit on neutral training only. All four captured blocks (8, 16, 24, 31), all three sites and all three seeds are retained for the canonical current/saved heads: 45 fits. The queried-value head is fit only after the query is visible at the query-line and pre-answer sites: 30 fits. No layer, seed or naming condition is chosen from new scores. Each fit reports the training prior, value accuracy, shuffled-control accuracy, class support, whole-pair accuracy and per-template development counts.

Every saved model is replayed on its complete fit/evaluation matrix using its saved normalization and weights. A separate audit reconstructs the grouped control mapping, requires exact histograms and complete fit coverage, and recomputes reported metrics from saved predictions. Value predictions are compared with the pinned original readout artifacts to make any numerical drift explicit. Capture chunks, old reference weights, original configurations and new analysis source have recorded content hashes. Interrupted fits resume from validated saved artifacts.

Frequency matching does not equate all task complexity. A random target assignment may be memorized on training bases and fail on new templates. Accuracy differences remain descriptive probe measurements, not evidence of natural causal use, information absence or a general binding register. The three seeds vary fit weights and shuffles; they are not independent dataset or model replications.

Configurations are `configs/binding/competition/matched_probe_all_slots_v1.json` and `matched_probe_queried_value_v1.json`. Source, job dependencies and completed reports are recorded under `artifacts/binding/matched_probe_controls/`. Only CPU probe fitting is required; both separately submitted GPU studies have also completed.
